Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

SkyBees AI (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information you provide when you contact us through our website.

Information we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the information you enter: your name, email address, industry selection, and message. We may also receive technical data that your browser sends automatically (such as basic server logs) depending on how the site is hosted.

How we use your information

We use your details only to respond to your inquiry, provide information about our services, and follow up if needed. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your contact form submission for marketing emails unless you explicitly ask us to (for example, by requesting a newsletter if we offer one).

Form delivery (FormSubmit)

Contact form submissions are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party service that forwards messages to our email. FormSubmit’s own terms and privacy practices apply to data processed on their systems. We recommend reviewing their documentation if you have questions about their handling of submissions.

Legal basis

Where applicable (for example under GDPR), we process your contact data based on your request and our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries.

Retention

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and for ordinary business records, unless you ask us to delete it and we have no overriding legal reason to retain it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at vivek@theskybees.ca.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
