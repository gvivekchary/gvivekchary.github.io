03 — Featured projects

Real problems, measurable impact

Deep cuts from Prime Air, industrial RF, and IoT security — problem → solution → impact → tech. Wording reflects real scope; customer names where appropriate.

Prime Air · Comms & VMS

Vehicle management & ground-link firmware

Problem
Autonomous aircraft need deterministic paths between vehicle management, radios, and ground — with safety partitions that do not cross-contaminate under fault.
Solution
Linux-centric comms stack with Ethernet, CAN, and NIC drivers on QorIQ / ST32, plus tightly scoped interfaces to DSP blocks and peripherals (ARINC 653-style thinking).
Impact
More stable end-to-end data exchange and lower effective latency in flight-critical loops; smoother integration of new sensors through disciplined driver boundaries.
Tech
C/C++, embedded Linux, CAN, Ethernet, NIC, Lauterbach, Agile CI/CT.

SYNQ · Imaging edge

Smart surveillance & EAS camera pipeline

Problem
Retail and security SKUs need reliable capture, transport, and OTA — on tight silicon and power budgets in the field.
Solution
End-to-end video pipelining on ESP32 / PSoC6: sensor bring-up, SerDes, capture timing, bootloaders, and remote diagnostics.
Impact
Higher uptime and image consistency in deployed gates and media devices; safer upgrades without bricked fleets.
Tech
C, RTOS/bare-metal, SPI/I2C/UART, WiFi, BLE, LoRaWAN, MQTT, HTTP.

Cattron · Industrial RF

Multi-MCU industrial remote platform

Problem
Material-handling remotes must stay responsive and safe across master, slave, and radio MCUs — with field updates that never strand a cab.
Solution
Architecture and drivers for real-time RF protocols, low-power sleep ladders, and OTA + config orchestration across the MCU mesh.
Impact
Predictable control under RF stress; faster qualification of new RF front-ends and safer rollout patterns for operations teams.
Tech
C/C++, SPI/I2C/UART/GPIO, multi-MCU, OTA, embedded Linux, Docker.

Drivers · Silicon bring-up

NIC, CAN & Ethernet hardening

Problem
Processor families differ in how they expose DMA, PHY quirks, and bus contention — “it works on the eval board” is not enough for production drones.
Solution
Low-level driver work on QorIQ and ST32-class parts: clean HAL boundaries, error recovery, and comms paths tuned with lab captures.
Impact
Fewer silent link drops under load; repeatable performance signatures for CI and flight-test sign-off.
Tech
C/C++, Ethernet, CAN, NIC, Linux kernel space adjacency, Lauterbach.

IoT · Connectivity fabric

Secure multi-radio device integration

Problem
Security products must mix BLE provisioning, WiFi backhaul, and LoRaWAN without opening attack surfaces or draining batteries overnight.
Solution
Unified driver + messaging layer tying radios to MQTT / XMPP / HTTP with consistent credential rotation and backoff policies.
Impact
Stable cloud attach rates in noisy retail RF environments; cleaner field diagnostics when links flap.
Tech
ESP32, PSoC6, ARM, MQTT, HTTP, BLE, WiFi, LoRaWAN.

Validation · Tooling

Lab-grade bring-up & CI/CT loop

Problem
Firmware teams lose weeks when lab evidence does not match what CI sees — especially across industrial and aerial environments.
Solution
Repeatable harnesses: Lauterbach trace plans, scope/signal-analyzer checkpoints, and Git + Docker pipelines that publish artifacts per board rev.
Impact
Shorter mean time to root-cause on timing bugs; higher confidence before OTA or flight campaigns.
Tech
Lauterbach, JTAG, oscilloscopes, Jenkins/CI, Docker, Agile.
